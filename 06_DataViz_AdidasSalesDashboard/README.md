# Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
Built an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing Adidas's recent sales growth, targeted at executives and marketing/sales teams. The analysis aims to identify the drivers behind Adidas's recent growth and derive a profit growth strategy that balances both scale and efficiency.

## KPIs
- **Primary KPI**: Operating Profit
- **Secondary KPI**: Operating Margin

Since Adidas already maintains a stable global revenue base, the focus was placed not on revenue itself, but on how much actual profit that revenue generates (Operating Profit) and how efficiently that profit is being earned (Operating Margin).

## Key Questions

**1. Why did Operating Profit change so sharply?**
- Operating Profit grew **+324% YoY in 2021** (up to 9x in June), while Operating Margin remained stable (~42.6%), showing no major shift in efficiency — the growth was driven by scale, not margin change.

**2. Why is there a regional imbalance between profit and margin?**
- **West**: High operating profit, lower margin → a scale-driven market
- **South**: Lower operating profit, highest margin → an efficiency-driven market

## Dimensional Analysis

**By Product (What)**
- Men's Street Footwear: highest profit and margin — the core product line
- Women's Apparel: highest margin (45.16%) — high-volume and highly efficient
- Women's Athletic Footwear: lowest profit but stable margin (42.63%) — an underleveraged, potentially high-value segment

**By Retailer (Who)**
- Amazon and Kohl's had little to no sales in 2020 but entered the market in 2021
- Foot Locker and Sports Direct generated the highest operating profit; Sports Direct ranked #1 in both profit and margin

**By Sales Method (How)**
- Online sales grew ~40x (accelerated by COVID-19), though in-store remained the largest contributor overall
- Cross-analysis of sales method by retailer showed that top performers (Foot Locker, Sports Direct) maintained strong performance across both online and offline channels — indicating stability and scalability rather than being purely online-driven

**By Region**
- California drove performance in the West; Texas drove performance in the South
- West: balanced sales across both high- and low-margin products
- South: highest profit from Women's Apparel (highest-margin product), but low contribution from Men's Street Footwear

## Insights & Action Plan

1. **Channel strategy**: Diversify from in-store-dominant performance toward the higher-growth online channel to secure long-term stability (e.g., Adidas's "reserve online, pick up in-store" system is a real-world example of this kind of integration).
2. **West region**: Shift focus toward high-margin products — particularly strengthening the women's product line — to improve efficiency in a market that is already large in scale.
3. **South region**: Expand the high-demand Men's Street Footwear line to grow scale in a market that is already efficient.
4. **Women's product line**: Given consistently strong margins across both Women's Apparel and Women's Athletic Footwear, this segment represents a high-potential area for investment — competitors (e.g., Nike's women's empowerment campaigns) have successfully grown this segment through emotional brand connection.
